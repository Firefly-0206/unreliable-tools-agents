\subsection{Loss in agent units (post hoc)}\label{app:units}
% src: raea4/agentenv/u_units.py -> results/u_units_{main,held,d2,d3}.json|txt; scripts/make_u_outputs.py
This post hoc analysis, like the effective-horizon analysis (\cref{app:horizon}), was computed on the stored logs without additional model calls. For every K2e episode of the main decomposition runs (CORE), the held-out confirmation and the DN parts of the second and third domains, the model's own trajectory, the exact planner on its stored JSON and the exact oracle act in the same world with the same pre-drawn tool outputs, and each outcome is scored against the true hidden state (\cref{tab:units}).
In the first domain no spend difference from the oracle is detected for the stronger models (model minus oracle: terra \uMainTerraCostDiffCI, Sonnet \uMainSonnetCostDiffCI). Their share of correct verdicts is lower in point estimate (\uMainTerraCorrect\% and \uMainSonnetCorrect\% against the oracle's \uMainTerraCorrectOracle\%), but the differences are not detected (\uMainTerraCorrectDiffCI\ and \uMainSonnetCorrectDiffCI\ points); Sonnet's realized loss exceeds the oracle's (\uMainSonnetRealisedDiffCI), and for terra no difference is detected (\uMainTerraRealisedDiffCI). Neither gives a false benign verdict in these episodes (flash: \uMainFlashSevere\%). On held-out episodes terra is correct in \uHeldTerraCorrect\% against \uHeldTerraCorrectOracle\% (\uHeldTerraCorrectDiffCI\ points); for Sonnet a difference is not detected (\uHeldSonnetCorrectDiffCI).
In the second domain Sonnet is correct in \uDtwoSonnetCorrect\% against the oracle's \uDtwoSonnetCorrectOracle\% (\uDtwoSonnetCorrectDiffCI\ points); for terra no difference is detected (\uDtwoTerraCorrectDiffCI), and terra spends more than the oracle (\uDtwoTerraCostDiffCI). In the third domain, where the stronger models avoid the outlets, no difference in correct verdicts is detected (terra \uDthreeTerraCorrectDiffCI, Sonnet \uDthreeSonnetCorrectDiffCI), and terra's loss shows as extra spend (\uDthreeTerraCostDiffCI). For the stronger models, fewer correct verdicts are therefore detected only for terra on held-out episodes and for Sonnet in the second domain (flash has fewer in the first and second domains); in the other terra and Sonnet sets no difference is detected, and terra's regret in the second and third domains also shows as extra spend.
Each realized outcome is a single draw per episode, so these intervals are wider than those of the exact regret. Terra's verdict regret is zero in every set and Sonnet's in all but the second domain (\ddSonnetVerdict\ there); where it is zero, each verdict is optimal given the evidence gathered, so extra wrong verdicts come from less informative evidence, not from misreading it.
