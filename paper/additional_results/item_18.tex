\paragraph{Exploratory pilot.}
The pilot used 18 configurations (three fault regimes, six evidence patterns), each in plain, warning and first-report-only presentations: 54 items, with three chat and two reasoner samples each. Only the latest complete parsed answer per item/model/sample was scored (270 answers from 480 attempts); reasoner caps were raised to 65,536 after truncation. In plain prompting, 7/10 suboptimal chat answers re-queried a source despite an accurate benign marginal; 2/54 overstated it and falsely declared healthy. Warning effects on optimality were inconclusive: +0.07 [-0.06, 0.22] for chat and +0.06 [-0.06, 0.17] for reasoner. First-report-only changes were -0.26 [-0.48, -0.06] and -0.25 [-0.50, 0.00]. This exploratory pilot motivated the benchmark.
