## Interpretation (hand-written for the runs at the time of writing: flash = deepseek-chat, terra = gpt-6.1-terra@medium, conditions P0/L1/L2, main and confirmation task sets; re-read after adding runs)

Verdict on the MMI-direction hypothesis: inconclusive, with weak support for flash only and no support for terra. The premise holds on the model side: MMI ranks a persistent-source repeat relatively too high (mean misvalue +0.34 [0.23, 0.45] loss units; it picks a repeat on 27% of repeat-available tasks where the exact optimum does so on 9%; confirmation set 24% vs 3%). Flash's net repeat bias has the MMI sign: it over-repeats (P0: 10% [4, 16] main, 12% [6, 19] confirmation; L1: 13% and 16%) and its bias in the share of repeat choices is positive (+6 [1, 12] and +11 [5, 17] points at L1), but this is about half of MMI's rate (22% [14, 30]) and fault-unaware's (20%), it largely disappears when the exact quantities are given (L2: 3% and 2%), and its under-repeat rate is high but rests on only 10 (main) and 3 (confirmation) tasks where a repeat is optimal (6/10 missed, CI [30, 90]), so a mixed "both directions" pattern cannot be excluded. Terra almost never errs on repeats (over-repeat 1% [0, 3] main, 4% [1, 8] confirmation, regret 0.02-0.03) and its errors are not MMI-like. The key test of whether the LLM errs where MMI errs is negative: the LLM falls in MMI's wrong category on only 2-14% of the tasks where MMI is wrong (84-99% where it is right), but that number is dominated by the LLM's accuracy; the accuracy-free statistic (share of the LLM's wrong answers on MMI-wrong tasks that are in MMI's category) is 50% at P0 and 38% at L1 for flash against 25% expected if it picked uniformly among the available non-optimal categories (14 and 13 wrong answers; PR is the commonest wrong choice, so the uniform baseline is crude), 17-50% for terra on 1-6 wrong answers, so flash's errors are somewhat MMI-like in category even though it errs rarely; the LLM's over-repeat rate is not reliably higher on tasks where MMI over-repeats (difference +0.11 [-0.05, +0.29], permutation p = 0.24 for flash P0; +0.05 [0.00, 0.16], p = 0.42 for terra; confirmation +0.02 and -0.05); flash's regret is higher where MMI is wrong at L1 (+0.13 [0.04, 0.24] main, +0.13 [0.02, 0.25] confirmation) but about as high on the fault-unaware placebo split (+0.07 and +0.10), and the pattern-stratified permutation tests do not separate them (p >= 0.16); the dose-response of flash's repeat errors on MMI's over-valuation is positive but borderline (rho +0.16 [-0.03, 0.33] P0, +0.21 [0.04, 0.38] L1, partial rho given the exact advantage similar; negative or null for L2 and for terra). At the pattern level flash's over-repeats concentrate on benign-repeat evidence (retry2, retry3, shared3, indep3), where MMI also over-repeats, but the across-pattern correlation is weak (+0.17, +0.22). Limits: one sample per task, temperature 0.7, 10 and 3 tasks with an optimal repeat, task-level bootstrap CIs that treat tasks as independent although they cluster in 12 evidence patterns (the report now also gives a pattern-cluster bootstrap CI, which is similar in width here, and the stratified permutation p; the permutation test is the authoritative one, and e.g. the fault-unaware P0 over-repeat contrast -0.12 has both CIs excluding 0 but permutation p = 0.33), and rank correlations that are fragile when the LLM makes only a handful of repeat errors (terra: 1 task).
